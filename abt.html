About CampusConnect

CampusConnect is a Student Placement and Internship Management System designed to connect students with job and internship opportunities.

The system helps students discover opportunities, apply for them and track their application status.

Purpose of the System

The purpose of CampusConnect is to make the placement and internship process simple, organized and efficient.

It provides a platform where students and placement officers can manage opportunities and applications.

Who Can Use CampusConnect?

🎓 Students

Students can create their profile, find opportunities, apply and track applications.

💼 Placement Officers

Placement officers can manage opportunities, applications and the placement process.

🛠️ Admin

Admin can manage users and monitor the overall system.

Why CampusConnect?

CampusConnect brings students and placement activities together in one organized platform.

It helps make the placement and internship process easier to manage.
